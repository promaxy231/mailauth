    How it works

    MailAuth reads public DNS records the same way receiving mail servers do. Everything runs in your browser — no backend, no logs of the domains you check.

    SPF

    Checks for a valid record, terminating mechanism, and estimates DNS lookups against the 10-lookup limit.

    DKIM

    Probes common selectors plus any custom selector you provide. Flags short or revoked keys.

    DMARC

    Validates policy strength, reporting addresses (rua), and subdomain policy.

    MX

    Confirms the domain can receive mail and that MX hosts resolve.

    FAQ

    Is this really free and private?

    Yes. Lookups go directly from your browser to Cloudflare’s public DNS-over-HTTPS. We do not store the domains you check.

    Why didn’t it find my DKIM key?

    Many providers use custom selectors. Enter yours in the “Custom DKIM selector” field and run the check again.

    What does the SPF lookup limit mean?

    SPF allows at most 10 DNS lookups (includes, a, mx, etc.). Exceeding it causes a permanent error and can break delivery.

    Does a high score guarantee inbox placement?

    No. Authentication is necessary but not sufficient. Reputation, content, and engagement still matter.
